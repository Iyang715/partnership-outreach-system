# Tracker Columns (ClickUp at WeLaunch; Google Sheets works too)

Creator handle | Instagram profile URL | Country (Instagram "About this account") | Follower count | Followers checked date | Avg likes (last 10, excl. pinned) | Avg comments (last 10) | Partner check run | Creator niche | Relevant content URL | Audience fit | Fit score (/25) | Risks/unknowns | Public business contact route | Outreach status | Initial outreach date | Next follow-up date | Reply / outcome | Human review owner | Notes

Status options: To research; Needs verification; Qualified; Draft ready; Approved; Contacted; Follow-up 1 sent; Follow-up 2 sent; Replied; Agreed; Shipped; Delivered; Content received; Ads permission secured; Declined; Closed – no reply.

Log every status change, rate quoted, decline, shipment and blocker on the day it happens.

Use the spreadsheet for dynamic campaign data; keep reusable standards in GitHub. Never auto-mark a creator as contacted based on a drafted message.
