# Creator Research & Outreach SOP

1. Read and summarize the assignment brief. Extract hard constraints, deliverables, deadline and unknowns.
2. Review official brand site, products, shipping rules, target audience and prohibited claims.
3. Research a broader candidate pool before narrowing to qualified final leads.
4. Verify each candidate's account, country, approximate current followers, content and fit; include sources and checked dates.
5. Score on /25 rubric, then rebalance for required follower size mix, geographic spread and category diversity.
6. Build a source-linked tracker. Store working contacts in approved business system, not public files.
7. Choose the right template from the outreach playbook (`03-taste-and-voice/`, `04-outreach-templates/`).
8. Draft creator-specific email/DM and one follow-up using only verified content details.
9. Human-review all facts, tone, commitments, disclosure expectations and contact consent before outreach.
10. Send only after authorization. Update tracker with actual date/status, and follow up appropriately.
11. Log results, learnings and revised standards to this repo via a deliberate GitHub commit; sync linked files into Claude Project.
