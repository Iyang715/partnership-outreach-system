# Creator Research Criteria

## Hard checks (always follow the latest brief's exact wording)
- Pirate Gold pilot brief: 6 Instagram creators, good mix of big and small, **3 under 10K followers**, based in a country the brand ships to.
- Per creator the brief asks for: handle + link, country, follower count, **average likes and comments across the last 10 posts**, 1–2 line fit rationale, one partner-quality check (fake followers, brand safety or recent activity).
- Exclude creators the brand has already gifted (check the brand account's tagged posts).

## How to measure (validated 2026-10-29)
- **Country:** Instagram profile → ⋯ → "About this account" → "Account based in". Do not trust third-party lists, bios or accents.
- **Last 10 posts:** the 10 most recent posts by date. Exclude pinned posts (they show first on the grid but are often months old). Count collab posts that appear on the creator's grid.
- **Likes/comments:** open each post. Record both average and median; a single viral post can double the average.
- **Hidden likes:** if every post shows "Liked by X and others", check your own setting first (Settings → Like and share counts). If only one creator shows it, they hide likes — note it as a limitation.
- **Date checked** on every row.

## Partner-quality checks (pick the one that matters most per creator)
- Follower vs following ratio (following > ~50% of followers can signal follow-for-follow growth)
- Engagement relative to size and whether one outlier carries the average
- Comment quality (real conversation vs emojis/bots)
- Posting cadence over the last 30–60 days
- Brand safety (content mix, political/controversial posts)
- Paid-service signals ("DM to book") that change gifted expectations

## Suggested scoring (0–5 each, total /25)
1. Category and product relevance
2. Audience likely to value game nights / product
3. Content quality and believable integration
4. Evidence of real audience activity and recent posting
5. Specific personalization and collaboration feasibility

21–25 = priority; 16–20 = viable; ≤15 = reconsider. Scoring supports judgment; it does not replace the brief's hard requirements.

## Research hygiene
- Source link and checked-on date for each public claim.
- Third-party aggregators (Modash, HypeAuditor etc.) are for discovery only; re-verify every number on Instagram.
- If Instagram limits access, mark "Needs manual verification"; never guess.
