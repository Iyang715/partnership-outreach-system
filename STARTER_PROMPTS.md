## 7. Self-review / QA
Audit all final research rows, links, dates, creator counts, brand claims, shipping facts, and personalized outreach. Label verified vs unverified items. Review the output from the perspective of a hiring manager receiving a 3–4 hour take-home task.

## 8. Repository improvement
