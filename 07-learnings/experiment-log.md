# Experiment & Improvement Log

Note: never portray hypothetical outcomes as actual campaign results. Entries below are research and process lessons, not outreach results.

---

**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Brand / campaign:** Pirate Gold Poker — Partnerships Coordinator take-home (research + outreach)
**What we tried:** Claude with a logged-in browser for creator discovery and per-post measurement; brand-site verification; Instagram "About this account" for country.

**What worked**
- Starting from the brand's own tagged posts surfaced 10 existing partners to exclude, and showed the brand already favours lifestyle/comedy creators.
- Forum research (Poker Chip Forum) changed the targeting: casual game-night and tabletop creators over poker pros.
- Checking country in-app caught an error: a creator on a UK "accounts to follow" list is US-based.
- Reading creator captions found a real hook (a bluffing / hidden-movement post) that ties to poker without forcing it.

**What failed or was unclear**
- The brand's listed Instagram handle does not exist.
- AI page-readers reported "Sold out" from a hidden template label; the product data said available.
- All likes looked hidden until the viewer account's "Hide like counts" setting was switched off.
- Instagram keyword search returns mostly shops/apps; curated articles and brand-collab networks (e.g. Chip Theory Games' tagged posts) were better discovery sources.
- Shipping countries conflict between FAQ (4) and currency picker (26) — unresolved, flagged.

**Updated rules**
- Country = Instagram "About this account" only. (02-research-criteria)
- Last-10 averages exclude pinned posts; report median beside average. (02-research-criteria)
- Check the brand's tagged posts for existing partners before shortlisting. (02-research-criteria)
- Gift Pirate Gold sets by default; John Wick needs licensor clearance. (01-brand-profiles)
