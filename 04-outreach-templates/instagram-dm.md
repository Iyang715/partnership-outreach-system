# Instagram DM Templates

Source: Outreach Playbook: Creator DM & Email Templates (Iyah, 29 Oct 2026, v2). Principles and the pre-send check live in `03-taste-and-voice/outreach-style-guide.md`.

**Rules**
- Fill every [bracket].
- Keep each DM short enough to read on one phone screen.
- The offer always names the content we'd like in return. That is what keeps it a collaboration and not a free gift.

---

## DM 1: Collaboration pitch

```
Hi [Name]! We loved your [specific post or video], especially [one detail you
noticed]. Your [niche] content is such a fun watch.

I'm Iyah from [Brand]. We make [product], made for [audience] to [main
benefit].

We'd love to collaborate and send you a [product] to feature in your content.
Would you be up for it?
```

## DM 2: Seasonal collaboration

```
Hi [Name], your [specific post or series] really stood out to us, [why in a
few words].

I'm Iyah from [Brand], and we make [product] for [audience] that [main
benefit].

With [season or event] coming up, people are shopping for [category] and
gifts, and we'd love to see [product] featured in your content. Would you be
interested in collaborating with us?
```

## DM 3: After they say yes

```
Thank you, [Name], we're so excited!

We'll send you [what's included]. Once it ships, we'll share a short guide
with ideas other creators have used, but your own take is always welcome.

From your side, we'd ask you to:
- Tag us at @[brand handle] in your post
- Add #gifted to the caption

Could you send your shipping address and the platform you'll be posting on?
We'll keep you posted on timing.
```

If the deal includes partnership ads permission or raw files, confirm them here too.

## DM 4: Follow-up (if no reply)

```
Hi [Name], just bringing this back up in case it got buried. We also loved
[new post since your first message].

We'd still love to send you a [product] to feature in your content. Let me
know if you're up for it!
```

## Worked examples: Pirate Gold Poker

Built from public posts checked 29 Oct to 6 Nov 2026. None of these have been sent.

### DM 1: Collaboration pitch → @buryboardgames (Tom, UK)

```
Hi Tom! We loved your Sniper Elite post, especially sitting in silence while
the table plots how to catch you. That's a poker bluff in a nutshell.

I'm Iyah from Pirate Gold Poker. We make a poker set with 300 weighted metal
coins in a wooden case, made for game nights that feel like playing for
treasure.

We'd love to collaborate and send you a set to feature in your content, in
your own style. Would you be up for it?
```

Why it's strong: the opener quotes his own words, and the poker link comes from him.

### DM 2: Seasonal collaboration → @themidlandmeeple (Craig, UK)

```
Hi Craig, your top 10 games of all time post really stood out to us, two
lists side by side and only a couple of exact crossovers.

I'm Iyah from Pirate Gold Poker, and we make a poker set with 300 weighted
metal coins in a wooden case for game nights that feel like playing for
treasure.

With the holidays coming up, people are shopping for game-night gifts, and
we'd love to see the set featured in your content. Would you be interested
in collaborating with us?
```

Why it's strong: it names a detail only he and Matthew could have posted (two separate lists). The season is framed as his followers' reason to care.

### DM 3: After they say yes → Tom

```
Thank you, Tom, we're so excited!

We'll send you the Bundle of Loot: 300 metal coins, the Kraken dealer chip
and two pirate card decks in the wooden case. Once it ships, we'll share a
short guide with ideas other creators have used, but your own take is always
welcome.

From your side, we'd ask you to:
- Tag us at @pirategoldpokerusa in your post
- Add #gifted to the caption

Could you send your shipping address and the platform you'll be posting on?
We'll keep you posted on timing.
```

Why it's strong: it spells out exactly what's in the box, so there are no surprises. It names the real brand handle; the one on the website doesn't exist.

### DM 4: Follow-up → Tom (5 days later)

```
Hi Tom, just bringing this back up in case it got buried. Since you work in
a board game café, it seemed worth adding: the set seats up to 10 players,
so it could get a proper run at a game night there too.

We'd still love to send you a set to feature in your content. Let me know if
you're up for it!
```

Why it's strong: instead of a bare "bump", it adds a new reason that's true for him (his café job, from his bio).
