# Email Templates

Source: Outreach Playbook: Creator DM & Email Templates (Iyah, 29 Oct 2026, v2). Principles and the pre-send check live in `03-taste-and-voice/outreach-style-guide.md`.

**Rule:** the offer always names the content we'd like in return, and the creative stays with the creator.

---

## Email 1: Personal collaboration pitch

```
Hi [Name],

[A rare combination about them, or one specific observation], and the [theme
or angle] of what we make lines up with that so well we wanted to reach out.

We make [product], [one-line description with three concrete details]. It's
built to make [ordinary moment] feel like [something special].

We'd love to send you one to create a piece of content around, whether that's
[format idea 1], [format idea 2], or whatever fits your feed. The creative is
yours, since you know your audience best.

You can see it here: [link]

If you're up for it, just let me know the best address to ship to.

Iyah
```

## Email 2: Proof-led collaboration

```
Hi [Name],

Your [specific video or series] is exactly why I'm reaching out. [One line on
why it fits].

[Proof point, e.g. official partnership], and [result with a number, e.g.
first run sold out in X weeks]. We're now on [current status].

We're offering a select group of creators a complimentary [product] in
exchange for a video feature in your format, like [format idea 1], [format
idea 2], or [format idea 3].

It includes:
- [Item 1]
- [Item 2]
- [Item 3]

If you're open to featuring it, we'll ship one out along with supporting
assets (b-roll, specs, and the backstory).

Best,
Iyah
[link]
```

For licensed products (e.g. John Wick Poker), nothing is offered, shipped or published before licensor clearance.

## Email 3: Moving from Instagram to email

```
Hi [Name],

We reached out on Instagram around [month] and wanted to share the
collaboration here, in case email is easier.

[Specific observation about their content]. [Brand] makes [product], [one-
line description].

We're partnering with a small number of creators on short-form videos in
their own style, no scripted ads. You can see the product here: [link]

If this feels like a fit, we'd love to send one your way to feature. Let me
know if you're interested or have any questions.

Thank you,
Iyah
```

## Email 4: Business or event partner

```
Hi [Team name] team,

[Specific detail from their site or gallery]. [Why the product fits their
range of events].

[Brand] ([link]) makes [product], [two or three concrete details].

We'd love to send you one to use at your next [event type], and in return
we'd love any photos or clips from the night.

If that sounds interesting, let me know so we can talk about it.

Best,
Iyah
```

## Email 5: Concept pitch to an existing partner

```
Hi [Name],

Hope you've been doing well!

We're planning a [Brand] x [their channel] collaboration for [season or
campaign] and wanted to see if you'd like to bring the idea to life.

Here's the first concept: "[concept title]"
[Link to script]

[Status note, e.g. still pending final approval], but we wanted to share it
early. We're also open to your ideas on what would work best for your
channel.

Let me know what you think!

Best,
Iyah
```

## Worked examples: Pirate Gold Poker

Built from public posts and the brand site, checked 29 Oct to 6 Nov 2026. None of these have been sent. Brand claims used: 300 weighted metal coins, Kraken dealer chip, two decks featuring history's legendary pirates, handmade wooden case, up to 10 players, the Pozible crowdfund, and "Trusted by Over 50,000 Customers" (all from pirategoldpoker.com).

### Email 1: Personal pitch → @boardandshield (Zeshawn, US; business email in his bio)

Subject options: "Game night, fatherhood edition" · "A poker set for your next game night"

```
Hi Zeshawn,

Your "board gaming does not stop for fatherhood" Reel is exactly the kind of
game night we had in mind when we made our set, and that lines up so well
with what we do that we wanted to reach out.

We make Pirate Gold Poker, a poker set with 300 weighted metal coins, a
Kraken dealer chip and two decks featuring history's legendary pirates, all
in a handmade wooden case. It's built to make an ordinary poker night feel
like splitting treasure.

We'd love to send you one to create a piece of content around, whether
that's a first-look Reel, a game night with friends, or whatever fits your
feed. The creative is yours, since you know your audience best.

You can see it here: pirategoldpoker.com/products/bundle-of-loot

If you're up for it, just let me know the best address to ship to.

Iyah
```

Why it's strong: the opener comes from one of his best-performing recent Reels (53 comments), and it names three concrete product details.

```
We're offering a select group of creators a complimentary set in exchange for

Reel, or an unboxing.

- 300 weighted metal coins in four finishes

Iyah
pirategoldpoker.com
```

Why it's strong: the proof is the brand's own, and the format ideas use his real series name. Only send by email if you find a public business email; otherwise use the DM.
