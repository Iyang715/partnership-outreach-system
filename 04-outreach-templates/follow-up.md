# Follow-up Templates

Source: Outreach Playbook: Creator DM & Email Templates (Iyah, 29 Oct 2026, v2).

**Rules**
- Keep follow-ups short and restate the offer. The strongest ones add a new detail about the creator.
- Send at most two, then close the lead out in the tracker (Follow-up 1 sent → Follow-up 2 sent → Closed – no reply).
- Never follow up if they declined or asked not to be contacted.
- For Instagram, use DM 4 in `instagram-dm.md`.

---

## Follow-up 1 (add a new detail)

```
Hi [Name],

Bringing this back up in case it got buried. [New detail about them, e.g. a
recent post or the range of their work], which made the fit feel even
stronger.

We'd still love to send you a [product] to feature in your content, in your
own style. You can see it here: [link]

Let me know if you're interested so we can talk about it.

Best,
Iyah
```

## Follow-up 2 (short reminder)

```
Hi [Name],

Just checking in on my note about [product]. The offer still stands: a
[product] for you to feature in your content, with full creative freedom.

Let me know if there's any interest.

Best,
Iyah
```

## Worked examples: email follow-ups → @boardandshield (Zeshawn), after Email 1

```
Hi Zeshawn,

Bringing this back up in case it got buried. I noticed you're also reaching
45K+ people on TikTok, which made the fit feel even stronger, since the set
is made to be shown off on camera.

We'd still love to send you a Pirate Gold Poker set to feature in your
content, in your own style. You can see it here:
pirategoldpoker.com/products/bundle-of-loot

Let me know if you're interested so we can talk about it.

Best,
Iyah
```

Why it's strong: the new detail (45K+ on TikTok) comes from his bio, so it's a real reason, not a bump.
