# Outreach Taste & Voice Guide

Source: Iyah's "Outreach Playbook: Creator DM & Email Templates" (v2, 29 Oct 2026). The full templates are in `04-outreach-templates/`; this file keeps the principles.

## How the messages work
Every message has four beats:
1. a specific note about the creator
2. a one-line product intro
3. a collaboration offer
4. one easy question

The tone is warm and short, and leaves the creative to the creator.

| Beat | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Personal opener | Names something real from their content: a case opening, a deck breakdown, a relatable video, or a rare mix of interests. |
| Product in one line | Says what it is, who it's for and, in the best messages, the benefit. |
| Reason now | Ties the message to a moment, like Black Friday, when people are shopping for gear and gifts. |
| The offer | A product sent for a video feature, short-form content in their own style, or a concept to bring to life together. |
| Creative freedom | "You know your audience best". No scripted ads; ideas from other creators are offered only as inspiration. |
| Support | Assets like b-roll, specs and the backstory, plus help with anything else they need. |
| One easy ask | A yes/no question, or a request for the shipping address. |
| After a yes | Confirm the tag, the disclosure hashtag, the platform they'll post on, and the address. |
| Follow-ups | Short, restate the offer; the strongest ones add a new detail about the creator. |

**The offer always names the content we'd like in return. That is what keeps it a collaboration and not a free gift.** This also matches WeLaunch's KPI: "Content received on brief and on time, with partnership ads permission secured."

## Quick check before sending
- [ ] The opener names something specific from this creator's content
- [ ] The product is described in one line, with what it does for them
- [ ] The message says we want them to feature it in their content
- [ ] Creative freedom is stated
- [ ] There is one easy question or next step
- [ ] After a yes: tag, #gifted, platform and address are confirmed
- [ ] Follow-ups are short and add something new when possible

## Habits worth keeping
- Before writing, spend two minutes on the creator's profile and note one detail that surprised you. That becomes the opener.
- Test the opener: swap in another creator's name. If it still works, it's too generic.
- Concrete details beat adjectives ("300 weighted metal coins", "wooden case").
